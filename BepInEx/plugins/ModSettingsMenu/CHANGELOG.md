### v1.1.4
#### 中文
- 修复离开模组设置后，设置被恢复成默认值的问题。
- 游戏更新导致本模组初始化失败时，硬依赖本模组的其他模组仍会加载。
#### English
- Fixed settings going back to default after you leave the mod settings menu.
- If a game update makes this mod fail to initialize, other mods that hard-depend on it will still load.

#### English
- Fixed mod settings failing to open in the main menu and pause menu after the game update.
